# Vignette 4 — The Traveler That Traveled by Email
**Duration:** 10 minutes | **Persona:** Sales coordinator, Ops

## Narrative Setup
> "When a Veloce system ships to a customer, it needs a 'traveler' — a tracking document that follows the order through compliance, logistics, shipping, goods receipt, and installation verification. Today, that traveler is a SharePoint document that gets emailed between teams. Version control is nonexistent. Let's see what an automated, system-tracked traveler looks like."

## Click Path

### Step 1: Order Travelers View (4 min)
**Navigate to:** `/orders/travelers`

**Talk track:**
- "Instead of a SharePoint document emailed between five teams, every order has an automated traveler."
- Point to the **stage legend**: Completed (green), In Progress (teal), Pending (gray).
- Walk through one traveler card in detail:
  1. **Order Received** — "The agreement is created. Timestamped, documented."
  2. **Export Compliance** — "Automated compliance checks run immediately."
  3. **Logistics Booked** — "Shipping is coordinated through the system."
  4. **Shipped** — "Tracking is updated automatically."
  5. **Goods Receipt** — "The customer confirms receipt in the system."
  6. **Install Verified** — "Field Service verifies the installation."
- Point to the **animated pulse** on the current stage: "Everyone can see exactly where this order stands."

### Step 2: Compare Multiple Orders (2 min)
**Still on:** `/orders/travelers`

**Talk track:**
- Scroll through the list.
- "Look — every active order has its own traveler. Some are further along than others."
- Point to a **Fulfilled** order: "This one is complete — all six stages done. That's the audit trail."
- Point to one still **in progress**: "This one is in logistics — Shari knows it will hit her books next period, not this one."
- "The bottleneck is always visible. No more 'who has the traveler?' emails."

### Step 3: Back to Compliance for Context (2 min)
**Navigate to:** `/orders/compliance`

**Talk track:**
- "Remember those compliance checks? They run as part of the traveler workflow."
- Expand the same order from Step 1.
- "Each check has a documented pass/fail/pending status."
- "For auditors, this is gold — automated compliance with a system-generated audit trail."

### Step 4: Agent Assist (2 min)
**Open:** Agent Chat

**Talk track:**
- Type: **"What's the status of the loan-to-sale for NVIDIA?"**
- "The agent can tell you the exact stage of any order's traveler — without navigating to the page."
- "For Kendrick in ops, this means he can check order status from his phone while on the floor."

## Key Messages
- The traveler is now a system-tracked workflow, not a SharePoint document
- Every stakeholder sees real-time status without email
- Compliance checks are automated and documented
- The audit trail is system-generated — no manual assembly
- Bottlenecks are visible immediately

## Transition to V6
> "The traveler solves order tracking. But what happens when something breaks? A blade fails, a customer is down, and five people scramble. Let's see what closed-loop automation looks like."
