# Vignette 5 — The Platform That Connects It All
**Duration:** 10 minutes | **Persona:** All stakeholders

## Narrative Setup
> "Today, every stakeholder in HAV operations has a different version of the truth — operations tracks assets in spreadsheets, finance reconciles revenue in Excel, the order pipeline lives in SharePoint. What if every stakeholder had a single, connected view — built on the data they already have?"

## Click Path

### Step 1: Dashboard Home (2 min)
**Navigate to:** `/` (Dashboard)

**Talk track:**
- "This is the HAV Operations Dashboard — a single pane of glass for the entire emulator fleet."
- Point out the **hero metrics banner**: Total Active Assets, Critical Alerts, Average Utilization.
- Show the **6 KPI cards**: Total Assets, Active Assets, Avg Utilization, Open Work Orders, Critical Alerts, Revenue Estimate.
- "Every number here is live from Salesforce — no spreadsheet intermediaries."

### Step 2: Rack Occupancy Chart (1 min)
**Still on:** Dashboard

**Talk track:**
- Point to the **Rack Occupancy by Location** bar chart.
- "Russell, you can see at a glance which colocation facilities are nearing capacity."
- Note color coding: red (>85%), amber (>70%), teal (healthy).

### Step 3: Telemetry Signals (1 min)
**Still on:** Dashboard

**Talk track:**
- Point to the **Telemetry Signals** table.
- "These are live signals from the emulator fleet — errors, warnings, idle systems."
- "In the current state, Ken has to check each system manually. Here it's aggregated and prioritized."

### Step 4: Contract Renewals (1 min)
**Still on:** Dashboard

**Talk track:**
- Point to **Upcoming Contract Renewals** table.
- Note the "AI Monitored" badge.
- "Shari, the system flags contracts expiring within 30 days in red, 90 days in amber."
- "No more quarter-end surprises from expired loans."

### Step 5: Navigate to Key Views (3 min)
**Navigate through sidebar quickly:**

1. **Asset Fleet** (`/assets`) — "Every Veloce system, serialized, with customer allocation and utilization."
2. **Capacity** (`/capacity`) — "Rack occupancy, power draw, PUE, and demand forecasts."
3. **Financials** (`/financials`) — "Revenue by product, lease type breakdown, repair costs."
4. **Work Orders** (`/workorders`) — "Every RMA, preventive maintenance, and break-fix — all tracked."
5. **Orders** (`/orders`) — "The full order pipeline from agreement to fulfillment."

**Talk track for each:** Keep it to one sentence per view — the goal is to show breadth, not depth.

### Step 6: Agent Chat Teaser (2 min)
**Open:** Agent Chat (sidebar button)

**Talk track:**
- "And when you don't want to navigate — just ask."
- Click **Fleet Status** suggested prompt.
- "The Agentforce agent has access to the same data. It can answer questions about assets, capacity, revenue, work orders — in natural language."

## Key Messages
- Every stakeholder sees their view — no spreadsheet intermediaries
- One data model powers everything
- Real-time, not quarterly
- AI-powered agent for instant answers

## Transition to V6
> "One connected platform is the foundation. Now let's see what it protects. In an Emulation-as-a-Service business, the revenue isn't the sale — it's the renewal and the expansion. Let's look at what happens when the signals that protect that revenue are scattered across four systems, and the renewal nobody saw coming lands on the account team's desk."
