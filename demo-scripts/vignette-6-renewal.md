# Vignette 6 — The Renewal That Nobody Saw Coming
**Duration:** 15 minutes | **Persona:** Account Team, Miriam (Head of Finance)

## Narrative Setup
> "Emulation-as-a-Service is a relationship business. The revenue isn't the sale — it's the renewal, the expansion, and the multi-year commitment. But the signals that protect that revenue are scattered: contract dates in one system, utilization in the capacity spreadsheet, support history in cases, margin in finance's month-end reconciliation. No one owns the whole picture of an account. So the renewal drifts toward expiration and the account team finds out it's at risk when the customer mentions they're evaluating alternatives — not months earlier, when a proactive conversation could have secured it. Let's see what a connected account 360 changes."

## Click Path

### Step 1: Contract Renewals (4 min)
**Navigate to:** `/accounts`

**Talk track:**
- "This is the account 360 — every account with its contracts, deployed and spare assets, utilization, support cases, and margin in one place."
- Point to the **renewal / expiration signals**: "Here's the renewal picture. The system knows which agreements are approaching expiration — before anyone builds a slide."
- Call out an account approaching renewal: "This EaaS agreement is drifting toward its expiration date. In the current process, the account team wouldn't know it's at risk until the customer raised it. Here it surfaces months ahead — time to have the proactive conversation."
- "Notice what's on this one record: the contract, the capacity the customer actually uses, the support history, and the profitability of the relationship. The account team acts on one picture, not four exports."

### Step 2: Idle Capacity → Expansion Signal (3 min)
**Still on:** `/accounts`

**Talk track:**
- "The mirror image matters just as much. Accounts running well below the capacity they're paying for are prime expansion candidates — or churn risks if they decide they're over-provisioned."
- Point to a **low-utilization** account: "This customer is using far less than their installed fleet. Today, under-utilization only shows up if someone manually cross-references usage against contract terms, account by account. Here it's a flag on the dashboard."
- "That's an expansion conversation sitting in plain sight — or an early churn warning. Either way, the account team sees it and acts on it instead of discovering it in a hallway."

### Step 3: Renewals in the Financial View (3 min)
**Navigate to:** `/financials`

**Talk track:**
- Scroll to the **contract renewals** due in the next 90 days.
- "Miriam, this is the recurring-revenue picture from your side — renewals quantified against actual usage and margin, not estimated at quarter close."
- "Renewal risk and expansion upside show up as numbers on a dashboard, tied to the same account signals the sales team is working. Finance and the account team are looking at the same truth."

### Step 4: Agent Assist (3 min)
**Open:** Agent Chat

**Talk track:**
- Type: **"List all contract renewals expiring in the next 30 days."**
- "The agent watches every account continuously — it surfaces the expiring agreements without anyone pulling a report."
- Follow up: **"Which customers have the lowest utilization on their installed fleet?"**
- "And it finds the idle-capacity upsell candidates the same way. Two questions, two proactive moves — before the customer raises either one."

## Key Messages
- Renewal risk surfaces months earlier — before the customer raises it
- Idle-capacity expansion signals come from the system, not manual cross-referencing
- One account view: usage, contract, support, and margin per customer
- Renewal and expansion opportunities are generated from the same signals that raised the flag
- The pre-meeting scramble to assemble a picture from four systems is gone — the review starts from insight

## Transition to V7
> "Renewals and expansions protect the revenue. But the operation underneath has to run without heroics. Let's see the full closed loop — a blade fails at a customer site, and the system detects, diagnoses, dispatches, and captures the cost, end to end."
